## Illustrative success signals — targets not approved
- Create a request with a title, category and description.
- See a status timeline for an authorized request.
- Reject cross-user access attempts.
- Demonstrate one approval flow with explicit policy decisions.

Do not convert proposed metrics into binding SLA commitments.
