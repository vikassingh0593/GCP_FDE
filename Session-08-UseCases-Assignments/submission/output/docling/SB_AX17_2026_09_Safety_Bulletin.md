# HelixNova Safety Bulletin SB-AX17-2026-09

Status: APPROVED - HIGH AUTHORITY

Effective: 07-Oct-2026 10:00 CET

Products: AX-17

Jurisdictions: Germany, Singapore, UAE

Subject: Temporary hepatic monitoring enhancement

Baseline hepatic laboratory values are required with repeat testing between Day 14 and Day 21. This bulletin does not change the approved German renal starting dose of 25 mg once daily for moderate renal impairment.
