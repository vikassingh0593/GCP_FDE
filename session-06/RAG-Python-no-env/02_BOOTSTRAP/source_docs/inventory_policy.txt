TREDENCE RETAIL INVENTORY DECISION POLICY
Version: 2026.3
Status: APPROVED

1. Inventory Freshness
For operational recommendations, an online store inventory snapshot must be no older than 15 minutes at decision time.

2. Offline Store Exception
When a store is confirmed offline, an inventory snapshot may be used for a qualified recommendation for up to 45 minutes. The recommendation must explicitly disclose that the offline-store exception was applied.

3. Reload Governance
If inventory evidence is stale beyond the permitted threshold, an inventory reload may be requested. A reload is a governed Data Engineering action and requires human approval before execution.

4. Stockout Decision
Stockout assessment should consider available inventory, projected demand and eligible inbound shipments. Available inventory is on-hand inventory minus reserved and damaged units.

5. Unsupported Actions
Inventory transfers between stores are not automatically authorized by this policy. A transfer requires a separately approved operational workflow.
