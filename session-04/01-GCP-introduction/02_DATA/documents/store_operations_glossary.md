# Store Operations Glossary

## Stockout Exposure
Stockout exposure means the operational risk that available inventory will fall below zero during the defined planning horizon.

## Fresh Evidence
Fresh evidence is evidence that satisfies the freshness requirement of the governing operational policy.

## SKU Identifier
A stock keeping unit may be referenced by exact identifiers such as `SKU-0921`. Exact identifiers should be preserved during retrieval and citation.
